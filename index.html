WLED Firmware Flasher

Flash our custom WLED builds to an ESP32 or ESP8266 over USB, straight from the browser.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.
  1. Connect the ESP with a USB data cable.
  2. Click Install for the firmware you want and pick the serial port.
  3. The matching build (ESP32 or ESP8266) is selected automatically.
  4. Choose Erase device when switching between major WLED versions (required for ESP32 0.13/0.14 → 16+).
